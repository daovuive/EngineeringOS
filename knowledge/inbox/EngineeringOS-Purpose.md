# EngineeringOS Purpose

EngineeringOS is a local-first engineering knowledge platform. Its purpose is to preserve and organize engineering knowledge, design experience, architecture decisions, lessons learned, and reusable assets. It uses local AI to search, analyze, connect, and reason over this knowledge while keeping authoritative source documents under user control. Canonical source: the EngineeringOS root README.

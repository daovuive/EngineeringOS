# Senior Product Owner – Autonomous Mobility & Fleet Management

> Source: CMC Global Job Description provided by user.

## 1. Position

- Company: CMC Global
- Position: Senior Product Owner – Autonomous Mobility & Fleet Management
- Type: Full-time Contract
- Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
- Client: German deep-tech company specializing in autonomous driving and intelligent mobility.

## 2. Opportunity

Strategic Product Ownership role for a Level 4 autonomous driving and fleet-based mobility product.

The role goes beyond traditional backlog management and includes ownership of:
- Product Vision
- Product Strategy
- Product Roadmap
- Customer Discovery
- Business Outcomes

Close collaboration with Engineering, Architecture, UX, Data and Operations.

## 3. Key Responsibilities

### Product
- Define and communicate product vision and strategy.
- Own roadmap and prioritization framework.
- Own product KPIs.
- Translate business and operational challenges into scalable product capabilities.
- Define measurable success metrics and value realization targets.
- Balance customer needs, technical feasibility and business value.

### Customer & Stakeholders
- Lead customer discovery sessions and workshops.
- Drive alignment across customers, business stakeholders and engineering teams.
- Work directly with international customers and senior stakeholders.
- Manage stakeholders, negotiation and decision-making.

### Leadership
- Act as a key domain expert for fleet operations and autonomous mobility.
- Mentor BAs, POs and delivery teams.
- Influence cross-functional teams without formal authority.

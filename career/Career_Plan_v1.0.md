**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** Active

## 1. Career Vision

Build a career at the intersection of **Engineering + Automotive + Architecture + Product + Business + AI**.

> Become the person who can transform complex customer/business problems into clear, scalable technical solutions and align customers, business stakeholders and engineering teams around those solutions.

## 2. Target Career Paths

2. **Technical Consultant / Customer Solution** — strong alternative / bridge
3. **Senior Feature Owner** — near-term path when it provides increasing scope

## 5. Immediate 2-Month Plan

### Primary: Foundation Architect

Choose as the primary 2-month course when it substantially covers:
- System / Software Architecture
- Architecture Decisions
- Quality Attributes
- Trade-offs
- Stakeholder concerns
- Architecture documentation
- System design

Goal: build the architecture thinking required for the Solution Architect path.

### Secondary: PSPO I

Useful for Product Ownership foundation and CV credibility, but **not the main solution to the current strategic gap**. Product Strategy, KPI, Business Case and Customer Discovery are higher-priority capability gaps.

## 6. Weekly Learning System — ~5 hours/week

- **2h Architecture:** Foundation Architect, Architecture Decision, Quality Attributes, trade-offs
- **1h AI for Engineering:** requirement review, architecture/design review, code review, test strategy, documentation
- **1h Executive Communication:** 30-second summary; Problem → Impact → Options → Recommendation
- **1h Architecture Portfolio:** write one ADR/week from a real engineering problem

## 7. Architecture Portfolio

Build a knowledge base of real ADRs containing:
- Context
- Problem
- Constraints
- Options
- Decision
- Rationale
- Trade-offs
- Risks
- Consequences
- Future impact
- Lessons learned

Target: **1 ADR/week ≈ 50 ADRs/year**.

Objective: demonstrate architecture thinking through real decisions, not certificates alone.

## 8. AI Strategy

Optimize for **AI-assisted Engineering**, not prompt engineering alone.

Use AI to increase leverage in:
- Requirements
- Architecture
- Design
- Testing
- Documentation
- Code review
- Knowledge management

Long-term positioning: **Engineer + Architect + AI**.

## 9. Product & Business Upgrade

Move from:

**Feature → Delivery**

to:

**Problem → Customer Value → Business Value → KPI → Priority → Roadmap → Outcome**

Learn to answer:
- What problem are we solving?
- Who has the problem?
- Why does it matter?
- What is the business impact?
- How will success be measured?
- Why should it be prioritized?
- What is the expected value?
- What is the cost of not doing it?

## 10. Career Decision Framework

Before accepting a role or course, ask:

### Strategic Fit
- Does it move toward one of the 3 target paths?
- Does it increase scope of influence?

### Capability Growth
- Does it build a capability difficult to replace with AI?
- Will I make architecture, business or customer decisions?

### Market Value
- Will the experience remain valuable in 3–5 years?
- Does it strengthen differentiation?

### Practical Value
- Can it be applied within 6 months?
- Is the ROI worth the time and money?

## 11. Certification Rule

> **Capability first. Certification second.**

For every certification ask:
1. Does it create a new capability?
2. Does it support the target career path?
3. Can it be applied to real work?
4. Does it improve market positioning?
5. Is its ROI higher than spending the same time on practical experience?

## 14. Career Risks / Blind Spots

These are career-development risks, not diagnoses.

- **Over-analysis:** practice decisions with incomplete information; record assumptions and revisit them.
- **Achievement attachment:** measure capability and impact, not certificates.
- **Responsibility overload:** separate personal accountability from distributed/systemic causes.
- **Excessive detail:** practice Conclusion → Why → Risk → Decision needed.
- **Avoiding difficult negotiation:** practice explicit trade-offs and decision ownership.

## 15. Personal Career Principle

> **Do not optimize for difficulty. Optimize for impact.**

Prioritize problems with high customer impact, business impact, system impact, learning value and strategic relevance.

## 16. Core Reminder

> **Do not try to become the person who knows the most. Become the person who helps everyone see what matters most.**

> **Do not learn only to feel valuable. Learn to solve problems that few people can solve.**

## 17. 6-Month Review

1. Am I closer to Solution Architect?
2. Am I solving larger problems than six months ago?
3. Are people asking me for decisions, not only implementation?
4. Can I explain technical decisions in business/customer language?
5. Can I define measurable outcomes for product decisions?
6. Can I make decisions with incomplete information?
7. Am I using AI to increase engineering leverage?
8. How many meaningful ADRs have I created?
9. Which capability increased my market value most?
10. Am I learning for capability or for validation?

## 18. Critical Principle

This plan is a **direction, not a prediction**. The market, AI and autonomous mobility may change.

> **Stay loyal to the capability strategy, not to a job title.**

Career compass:

**Engineering + Systems + Architecture + Customer + Business + AI**

The exact title can change; the capability direction remains the compass.

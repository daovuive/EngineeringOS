# Architect Daily Program

## Personal Learning Specification & Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0 **Owner:** Dao

This document captures the long-term learning specification.

## Vision

-   Become a Software Architect, Solution Architect, and stronger
    Product Owner.
-   Prepare for iSAQB CPSA Foundation.
-   Build architect mindset, systems thinking, architecture decision
    making, business thinking, and communication.

## Daily Learning Format (15 minutes)

1.  Learning objective
2.  Core concept (Vietnamese explanation)
3.  Deep explanation
4.  Architect mindset
5.  Real-world architecture example
6.  iSAQB mapping
7.  Mini exercise
8.  Reflection
9.  Summary
10. Flashcards
11. Keywords

## Learning Cadence

-   Day N: New lesson.
-   Day N+1: Review the same lesson with new examples and questions.
-   Day N+2: Next lesson.
-   No repetition after the second day unless explicitly requested.

## Knowledge Base Rules

-   Every lesson has an ID.
-   Every lesson links to previous and next lesson.
-   Every lesson is written in Markdown.
-   Every lesson is preserved as part of a long-term knowledge base.
-   The collection should be directly usable in NotebookLM or other PKM
    systems.

## Roadmap

## Milestones

-   Every 10 lessons: Review + Quiz + Mind Map.
-   Every 30 lessons: Mini Project.
-   Around 90 lessons: Mock CPSA Foundation.
-   Around 180 lessons: Capstone Architecture Project.

## Long-term Principle

The goal is to continuously build a personal architecture knowledge base
that supports both career growth and certification preparation.
